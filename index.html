The structural difference

Every other platform proves a negative. We removed the negative.

The self-reported approach

  • Data is uploaded by each supply-chain party, in whatever format and cadence suits them.
  • EUDR means proving no deforestation after 2020, which is inferred from plot polygons and satellite imagery.
  • Water and land figures are modelled estimates that nobody downstream can reproduce.
  • Sustainability claims rest on third-party spot audits of a small sample.

The closed-system approach

  • EC, pH, VPD, PPFD and CO₂ arrive from the chamber controller. Nobody types them in afterwards.
  • There is no agricultural plot, so land-conversion risk is zero by construction rather than zero by estimate.
  • Water and energy come from the batch record, so any buyer can recompute them from the same inputs.
  • Every harvest resolves to a named chamber and the climate series that produced it.
Provisional — closed-environment cultivation is more electricity-intensive than open field, and we show that leg as a deficit rather than hiding it. Water, land, agrochemical and deforestation legs move the other way.
