Why the deforestation question collapses

Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 asks a single hard question: was this commodity produced on land deforested after 31 December 2020? For an open-field plot the answer requires polygon mapping, satellite time series and a defensible risk assessment — a season of field work that still only supports an inference.

Aeroponic production has no agricultural plot. The production site is a building. The compliance question stops being "prove a negative about a forest you may never have visited" and becomes "point at a footprint and show the geolocation".

Being inapplicable is not the same as being exempt. The operator, the legality of the business and the chain of custody still have to hold up. This system files those as verifiable checks rather than paperwork.
