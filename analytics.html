Reading the numbers

Blue water — a closed loop recovers roughly 94% of recirculated water from condensate, so net withdrawal is under 2 L per kg green against a 250 L baseline.
Land — figures count floor footprint only. The rack surface actually under cultivation is several times larger, which makes the comparison conservative.
Energy — the deficit is real. A sealed environment pays for water and land savings in electricity, and at 0% renewable the carbon figure is worse than open field.
Deforestation — exposure is zero because no land is converted. This is the only leg where the number is structural rather than measured.
