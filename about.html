Traceability stops being a reporting problem when there is no land involved.

This is the argument the platform is built to make. It is also the argument that decides whether an EU importer can accept the coffee at all.

01 — The problem with self-reported origin

Everyone in the chain has a reason to shade the data.

A

Ingestion gap

A cooperative with four hundred smallholders reports volumes monthly on paper. By the time it is in a system it is an aggregate, and the aggregate is what everyone downstream relies on.

B

Inference gap

Deforestation-free status is inferred from plot polygons against satellite time series. It is a defensible inference, not an observation, and it only covers land someone mapped.

C

Reproduction gap

Water intensity and carbon are modelled from regional averages, so two buyers asking the same farm for the same number can get different answers and both be "correct".

None of this is dishonesty. It is what happens when the evidence is generated by the party whose revenue depends on it.
02 — What the closed system changes

Move the plant indoors and four of the five questions disappear.

Open-field supply chain

  • Plot geometry must be collected before a harvest season even starts.
  • Water use is a modelled function of rainfall, irrigation method and region.
  • Carbon depends on fertiliser regime, soil carbon and transport distance.
  • Agrochemical exposure is verified by certificate, periodically.
  • Chain of custody breaks at every commingling point.

Closed aeroponic system

  • No plot. The site is a building with a geolocation and a footprint.
  • Water is metered at the loop and recovered at the condenser.
  • Energy is metered at the meter, so carbon is arithmetic on a known grid factor.
  • No pesticide programme exists to verify.
  • One chamber, one batch, no commingling until packing.
Deforestation

Risk becomes structurally zero rather than estimated low. There is no plot to test against the cutoff date, and the facility can be inspected in person in an afternoon.

Water

Closed-loop recovery means net withdrawal under 2 L per kg green, from a meter reading, not a model. That is roughly 0.4% of the conventional blue-water baseline.

Energy

The honest weakness. Sealed environments consume more electricity than open field. At 60% renewable the network is carbon-negative against baseline; at 0% it is not, and the platform reports both.

03 — How the evidence is built

Four layers, each one only as strong as the one below it.

Layer 1 — identity

GrowerID

A grower, facility or chamber is an object with a decentralised identifier. Creation is an event. Editing is an event. There is no state that can change without a corresponding record, which means the history of a relationship is inspectable rather than reconstructed from memory.

Layer 2 — telemetry

8 channels

EC, pH, root-zone and air temperature, relative humidity, VPD, PPFD and CO₂. The raw stream is high-volume and stays in ordinary storage. What goes into the ledger is a periodic digest with the window, the sample count, the mean and the setpoint — enough to prove the climate a batch grew in, without drowning the chain in noise.

Layer 3 — ledger

sha256 chain

Each event hashes its own content together with the previous event's hash. Recomputing the chain from genesis detects any alteration at the exact sequence where it happened. Attestation and transparency are different problems: this system solves transparency, and states plainly that hardware attestation is the remaining gap for sensor honesty.

Layer 4 — compliance and consumer

EUDR + LCA

At harvest the system derives two dossiers from the batch's own record and anchors them. The same evidence serves an EU importer filing a due-diligence statement and a consumer scanning a bag on a kitchen counter. One body of evidence, two audiences, no re-keying.

05 — What is not finished

Limits stated before a buyer finds them.

Open gaps

  • Sensor honesty rests on the gateway, not yet on signed hardware.
  • Conventional comparison legs are literature medians, not measured competitors.
  • EU TRACES submission needs operator registration before it can be filed directly.
  • Carbon accounting is cradle-to-gate; use-phase and end-of-life are excluded.

Next

  • Signed sensor attestation at the chamber controller.
  • A consortium write model so partners co-own the ledger.
  • Settlement rails for grower payouts and consumer contributions.
  • Multi-language consumer pages, starting with Japanese for the Osaka facility.
